# Art direction — grand strategy demo

Mood: warm parchment war table, dusk.

- Background deep: `#141018`
- Surface panel: `#241d2b`
- Primary (your nation, land): `#4e7a4a`
- Accent (gold, UI, highlight): `#d9a441`
- Danger (enemy land, alerts): `#b4402f`
- Paper text: `#efe3cf`

Field: 1280x720 canvas, stretch canvas_items / expand.
Map grid 14x9 provinces drawn with code, no image files.
UI: 6px corner radius, HUD font 20, title 32, body 16.
Effects: subtle only — province select pulse, battle flash 120 ms, shake under 6 px.
