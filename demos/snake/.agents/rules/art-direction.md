# Art direction — Neon Grid

Mood: neon night arcade, dark plate + glowing lines.

- Background: #0B0E14 deep navy-black, subtle radial vignette
- Surface (board): #141A26 with #1E2A3D grid lines
- Primary (snake): #35E0A1 mint green, head #7CFFD4
- Accent (food): #FFC24B amber, glow pulse
- Danger (walls / crash): #FF4D6D red
- Field: 20x20 cells, drawn to fill ~70% of 1280x720 window; HUD bar on top, board never covered.
- UI: rounded panels (radius 10), HUD font 22, title 34, outline on all text.
- Effects: score pop + screen shake 6px on eat, red flash 120ms on crash.
