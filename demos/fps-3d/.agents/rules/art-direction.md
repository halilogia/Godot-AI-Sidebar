# Art direction

Mood: cool night-dusk, clean sci-fi lab. Procedural only, no image files.

Palette (use only these + tints/shades):
- background sky: #101a2b
- surface / floor: #2a3550
- primary: #4fc3f7 (player HUD, highlights)
- accent: #ffd166 (collectibles, goal)
- danger: #ef5350 (goal ring / feedback)

World: 40x40 m floor, 1.8 m eye height, subtle fog for depth.
UI: rounded panels (radius 10), HUD font 22, title 32, dark panel #101a2bcc,
text white with dark outline. Effects subtle: shake < 6 px, flash < 150 ms.
