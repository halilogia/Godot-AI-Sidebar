# Art direction
Mood: neon night, cool blue arena with warm gold accents.
Palette (res://scripts/Palette.gd):
- BG_DEEP #0b0f1a (arena floor base)
- BG_FLOOR #141b2d (floor gradient)
- GRID #1e2942 (grid lines, panels)
- SURFACE #243252 (UI panels)
- PRIMARY #4fd1c5 (player, bullets)
- ACCENT #ffd166 (score, pickups, aim reticle)
- DANGER #ef476f (enemies, damage)
- TEXT #e8f1ff
Field: 1280x720, camera static, arena fills screen.
UI: flat panels with 10px radius, HUD 22px, titles 40px, outline on all text.
Effects: hit flash 120ms, shake max 6px, no permanent particles.
