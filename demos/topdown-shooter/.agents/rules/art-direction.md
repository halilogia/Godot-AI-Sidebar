# Art Direction — Neon Bunker (top-down shooter)

Mood: cold neon night, high contrast, readable at a glance.

Palette (only these + tints/shades):
- Background / void: #0d1117
- Surface (floor, panels): #1b2430
- Primary (player, friendly): #4fd1c5
- Accent (muzzle, pickups, highlights): #f6c445
- Danger (enemies, damage): #f2545b

Field: 1280x720, camera fixed, arena is a rounded dark panel centred, ~75% of screen height.
UI: rounded panels (radius 10), HUD font 22, title 36, no default grey controls.
Effects: hit flash 100 ms, screen shake <= 6 px, death particles short, muzzle glow low.
Silhouettes: player = teal triangle (ship), enemy = red diamond, bullet = accent dot, pickup = yellow circle.
