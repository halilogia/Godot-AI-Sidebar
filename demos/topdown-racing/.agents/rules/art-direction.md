# Art direction — Neon night racing (top-down)
- Mood: neon night circuit, dark asphalt, glowing accents.
- Palette: bg #12141c, surface #262b3a, asphalt #3a4152, primary #4cc2ff, accent #ffd166, danger #ff5c6e. All colours live in res://scripts/Palette.gd.
- Camera: 1280x720, canvas_items stretch, expand aspect. Follow player, zoom 1.0, lookahead 60px.
- UI: custom Theme, panels corner radius 10, title 34, HUD 22, body 16, all text with outline.
- Effects: subtle. Screen shake < 6px, hit flash 120ms, off-track dust particles short-lived.
