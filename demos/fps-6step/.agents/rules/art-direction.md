# Art Direction — fps-6step

Mood: "cold test chamber" — desaturated blue-grey lab, clean and readable.

Palette (only these + tints/shades):
- Background/sky: #1b2026
- Surface (ground, walls): #39424c
- Primary (player, floor trim): #7fd4c1
- Accent (crates, glow): #f2b34c
- Danger (fall marker, damage): #e0553f

Rules:
- 1280x720, stretch canvas_items / expand.
- UI: corner radius 8, panel bg #1b2026 at 85% alpha, body font 18, title 32.
- Lighting: sky ambient 0.35 cool tint, one DirectionalLight3D warm-neutral with soft shadows.
- Effects subtle: no screen shake beyond 4px, no permanent particle loops.
