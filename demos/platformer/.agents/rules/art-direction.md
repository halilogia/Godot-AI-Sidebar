# Art Direction — Neon Dusk Platformer

Mood: neon dusk / synthwave (synthwave dusk, cool blues + magenta accents)

Palette (only these 5 + tints):
- BG_DEEP   #10131f  (sky top / vignette)
- SURFACE   #1e2540  (platform tops, panels)
- PRIMARY   #4fd1ff  (player, highlights)
- ACCENT    #ff5fa2  (pickups, goal, accent lines)
- DANGER    #ff5252  (enemies, hazards, damage)

Camera: 1280x720, canvas_items stretch, aspect=expand. Camera zoom 1.0.
Field: level is 60 tiles x 20 tiles of 64px = 3840x1280 world px.
UI: rounded 10px panels, HUD font 22, title 40.
Effects: shake < 8px, flash < 150ms, glow low (0.25).
